# Skill: Evaluate Deal & Draft Negotiation
- **Valuation Baseline**: Query KBB / Hagerty Private Party Valuation for 2009–2012 Cayman/Boxster.
- **Flag Criteria**: Price is >= 15% below market baseline AND title is Clean.
- **Max Target Budget**: Negotiate toward your target cap (e.g., $28,000–$35,000 depending on S vs Base trim).
- **Message Draft Rule**:
  "Hi [Seller], is this 987.2 [Cayman/Boxster] still available? Has it undergone recent maintenance (borescope / shift cables / AOS / water pump)? I'm a serious buyer looking to move quickly."
